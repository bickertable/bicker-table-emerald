Privacy policy

How Bicker Table Advisory handles personal information. Effective November 4, 2026.

Who we are

Bicker Table Advisory is the trade name of TBT Advisory LLC ("Bicker Table," "we," "us"), based in Palm Desert, California. This policy covers bickertable.com (the "Site") and the conversations and advisory work that start from it. Contact: jamie.arias@bickertable.com.

Information you give us

  • Your name, email address, and anything you include when you email us.
  • Information you submit when you book a call. Booking runs on Google Calendar. Google collects that information under its own terms, and we receive what we need to hold the meeting.
  • What you tell us about your organization, and materials you choose to share if we work together.

The Site does not host a contact form, and it does not run a text-message program. We do not ask you to opt in to marketing texts here.

Information collected automatically

The Site is static. It does not set its own analytics cookies. Your browser requests fonts from Google Fonts, and the booking page embeds a Google Calendar scheduler. Those requests can send technical data such as your IP address to Google. You can control cookies in your browser.

How we use information

  • To answer you, hold the pre-assessment, and do the advisory work you engage us for.
  • To prepare for a conversation, including reviewing information you send and publicly available professional information.
  • To keep ordinary business records and meet legal duties.

We do not sell your personal information.

How we use AI tools

A person directs this work. We may use third-party AI tools to support research, analysis, and drafting. We do not use confidential client material to train a model of our own. If a tool would receive confidential material, that is covered in the engagement, not assumed from a visit to this Site.

Who we share information with

We share information with service providers who help us operate, such as email, scheduling, and hosting, and only so they can provide that service. We may share it if the law requires it, or if the business is transferred. We do not share information with partners for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including ordinary legal, tax, and accounting duties. When we no longer need it, we delete it or de-identify it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, by emailing jamie.arias@bickertable.com. We may need to confirm it is you. California residents may also have rights to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Children

This Site is for organization leaders. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Work with a school is work with the organization, not a request for student data through this Site.

International visitors

We are in the United States. Information we collect is processed in the United States.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
