Who we are

Bicker Table Advisory is an executive advisory practice operated by [Legal entity name, e.g. Bicker Table Advisory LLC] ("Bicker Table," "we," "us"). This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information when you visit our website at bickertable.com (the "Site"), contact us, attend our events, or work with us.

02

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number, company and title when you fill out a form, book a call, register for an event or email us.
  • If you opt in to text messages, your mobile number and a record of your consent.
  • Information you share about your business, experience and goals during conversations and engagements.
  • Materials you provide to us as a client, such as documents, content, recordings and data.
  • Billing details. Payments are handled by our payment processor, and we do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage information such as browser type, pages viewed, referring pages and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

Information from other sources

  • Publicly available professional information, such as company websites, professional profiles and public directories, which we may review when preparing for a conversation or engagement.
  • Information from partners or referral sources who introduce you to us.
03

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries, schedule meetings and provide our services.
  • To send text messages you have agreed to receive, such as service notifications, meeting updates and replies from your representative.
  • To prepare assessments, research, content and other deliverables for clients.
  • To send updates, insights and event invitations, which you can opt out of at any time.
  • To operate, secure and improve our Site and services.
  • To process payments and maintain business records.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.
04

How we use AI tools

Our work is human directed and AI accelerated. We use third-party AI systems to support research, analysis, drafting and automation. Information you provide may be processed by these tools on our behalf, subject to their terms and data protections. A person on our team directs and reviews the work. We do not sell your information, and we do not use confidential client materials to train AI models of our own.

05

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as needed to run our business:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as website hosting, email, scheduling, customer relationship management, workflow automation, analytics, payment processing and AI tools. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Collaborators working on an engagement with us, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law or to protect rights, property or safety.
  • Business transfers, such as a merger or sale of assets, subject to this policy.
06

Text messaging and mobile information

If you check the consent box on our contact form, you agree to receive text messages from Bicker Table Advisory, including notifications about our services, updates on upcoming meetings and replies from your dedicated representative. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for assistance.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. This information will not be shared with any third parties.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or service.

07

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep our Site working, understand how it is used and improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the Site work.

08

We keep personal information for as long as needed for the purposes described here, including providing services, meeting legal, tax and accounting requirements, and resolving disputes. When it is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
