The Real Problem

Educational content demonstrates what you know. Authority IP demonstrates how you think.

Many experts have good, informative content covering their field, contracts, frameworks, best practices. The material demonstrates competence. It could often have been written by almost any competent peer in that field.

The problem is not that your content is bad. The problem is that it could belong to almost anyone.

Authority IP makes the expertise distinctly attributable to you.

The Thread Concept

You don't need one tiny niche. You need a coherent architecture.

Some experts have several legitimate bodies of expertise. The work is not forcing artificial sameness, it's establishing a coherent umbrella and building clear threads underneath it, so AI systems and humans encounter coherence instead of scatter.

How the Engagement Works

Seven stages. One coherent body of evidence.

Authority Discovery

Determine the expertise, patterns, frameworks, and intellectual territory you can credibly own.

Eminence Positioning

Define what you should become known for and how your different bodies of expertise connect.

Authority Architecture

Build the threads, pillars, and editorial territory that create a coherent body of evidence.

Authority IP Development

Develop roughly ten to twelve substantive cornerstone pieces from your actual thinking and experience, released at roughly one to two per week.

Distribution Architecture

Determine where the work needs to live and how it's reinforced through LinkedIn, relevant communities, social content, and other appropriate formats.

AI Visibility Benchmarking

Establish a baseline for how you currently appear across selected AI answer systems, and track change over time. Observational, not a guaranteed-ranking service.

Market Conversation

Upgrade DMs, profile positioning, engagement strategy, and calls to action so the authority work connects to real business conversations.
