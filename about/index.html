About

Jamie Arias

Founder and AI Systems Architect of Bicker Table Advisory. I'm an AI Systems Architect for organizations that run on people. I help established schools and service businesses in the US and UK move from staff using AI on their own to AI that works across the operation with people directing it.

Why this work

Individual AI productivity is not organizational intelligence.

I say that because I keep meeting organizations where people are plainly getting faster, and the operation is unchanged. The mission is not to hand human thinking over to AI. It is to use AI to think more clearly, communicate more precisely, and turn the knowledge already inside an organization into a way of working that people still direct.

Background

Jamie Arias is the founder of Bicker Table Advisory. She has completed three prompt-engineering and auditing projects with major AI companies, two with Google and one with Meta, and built the strategic frameworks the firm's advisory work runs on.

She spent 10 years teaching, including K-12, adults, and Title I middle grades, and 20 years in sales, including digital marketing for home health and real estate. She works in English and Spanish. Bicker Table Advisory is based in Palm Desert, California.

That mix is why the work sits with schools and with service businesses. I have been in the classroom and in the sales conversation. I am interested in how work actually moves through people, and in where AI can help that work next.

How I work

I use a six-step sequence: investigate, map, diagnose, prioritize, design, implement. Every recommendation has to be something a leadership team can carry, with people directing the result. When a first conversation shows a reason to go further, the Agentic AI Opportunity Brief is the discovery I use. It is an early look at the operation. It is not a technical audit and not an implementation plan.
